Bug-fix release: **the status bar now follows the window size.**

### Fixed
- **Status bar cut off after resizing the map or the window.** Games draw their status line for a fixed number of columns, and most Z-code games only redraw it on the next turn. Until then the score on the right was cut off, or a gap remained when the window got wider. Brass Lantern now lays out status bars itself: the room name stays on the left, score and moves stay right-aligned and centred titles stay centred – at any width and live while you drag the map divider.
- **Score not right-aligned with proportional fonts** (e.g. the default Lectrote style): games align the score with spaces, which are narrower in proportional fonts. This no longer matters with the new layout.
- Windows that reach the right or bottom edge now follow the window size immediately instead of being clipped until the game has redrawn.
